Research

I use continuous symmetries to study geometric structures on manifolds. My research can be divided into roughly two directions.

One part of my research lies at the intersection of Lie theory (in particular Lie algebroids) and formal partial differential equations, in particular those related to geometric structures ($G$-structures).

I also study Lagrangian submanifolds of Poisson manifolds. I am especially interested in the linearization problem of Poisson structures around Lagrangian submanifolds. Applications deal with Poisson groupoids.

In both research areas, local and global integrations of Lie algebroids to Lie groupoids play a central role.

Papers and preprints

  • Notes on relative algebroids

    Accepted for publication in the Springer INdAM Series · arXiv:2510.21987

    Introductory notes on relative algebroids: where they come from in classification problems, their prolongation and integrability theory, and their link with PDEs with symmetry.

  • Relative algebroids and symmetries of Pfaffian fibrations

    Preprint · arXiv:2510.01517

    Relates relative algebroids to Pfaffian fibrations: every Pfaffian fibration induces a relative algebroid with the same prolongations and local solutions, and actions of Pfaffian groupoids by symmetries respect it.

  • Relative algebroids and Cartan realization problems

    With Rui Loja Fernandes · Accepted for publication in Selecta Mathematica · arXiv:2503.19233

    Introduces relative algebroids as a Lie-theoretic framework for the existence and classification of geometric structures defined by PDEs, and shows that any PDE can be recast in this language.

  • Lie groups of Poisson diffeomorphisms

    Journal of Symplectic Geometry 21 (2023), no. 5, 889–937 · arXiv:2108.11490

    Uses Poisson groupoids to turn Poisson diffeomorphism groups into infinite-dimensional Lie groups, for example for log-symplectic, scattering-symplectic and cosymplectic manifolds.

  • Linearization of Poisson groupoids

    Indagationes Mathematicae 33 (2022), no. 3, 682–717 · arXiv:2108.11491

    Extends the Lagrangian neighbourhood theorem to cosymplectic Lie algebroids and uses it to show that dual integrations of triangular bialgebroids are linearizable around the unit section.
